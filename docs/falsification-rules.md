# Falsification Rules

1. Negative results are valid research results.
2. Do not tune experiments merely to make TFL win.
3. Ground truth must remain evaluator-only.
4. Raw results must be written before interpretation.
5. Exploratory and confirmatory runs must remain separate.
6. Confirmatory data must not be used for feature or threshold tuning.
7. No post-hoc feature invention after inspecting final labels.
8. Frozen predecessor experiments must never be modified.
9. Claims must not extend beyond what the experiment demonstrates.
10. Equivalent performance with substantially higher complexity is a NO-GO.
11. Out-of-family robustness is more important than in-sample wins.
12. Spectral features must earn their role empirically.
